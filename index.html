    Inspect the reconstructed hash path

    Membership is a claim.
    Trust is a separate choice.

    Leaves hash as SHA-256(0x00 || UTF-8 record). Internal nodes hash as SHA-256(0x01 || left hash || right hash). Uneven trees split at the largest power of two below their size; no leaf is duplicated to fill a level.

    The proof carries a record, its index, the tree size, and sibling hashes. The verifier derives left/right directions from the index and size, reconstructs the root, and checks it against your separate checkpoint.

    A matching inclusion proof does not identify a publisher, establish a timestamp, or prove that successive trees are append-only. This workbench does not implement signatures or consistency proofs.
